General notes

  • Late slots are bad. Anything from 14:20 eats the afternoon, and it's the slot for bums who don't study.
  • You pick a class type each term, so you can switch from Intensive A to Regular A after term 1.
  • B classes only appear as a 3rd choice on the form. If they don't fill, you're moved to A and pay the difference.
  • HES docks a month if your term average drops below 80.
